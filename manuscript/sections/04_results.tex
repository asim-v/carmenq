\section{Exact frontiers and resource separation}
\label{sec:results}

\begin{theorem}[Streaming parity audit--return frontier]
For the adaptive classical-memory null in \cref{sec:model}, including arbitrary non-QND local instruments and arbitrary transcript-conditioned joint recovery,
\begin{align}
\Cstream
&=\max_{0\leq t_1,\ldots,t_n\leq1}
\left[
\frac{\lambda}{2}\left(1+\prod_{i=1}^n t_i\right)
+(1-\lambda)\prod_{i=1}^n f(t_i)
\right] \label{eq:productfrontier}\\
&=\max_{0\leq t\leq1}
\left[
\frac{\lambda}{2}(1+t^n)+(1-\lambda)f(t)^n
\right].
\label{eq:frontier}
\end{align}
The bound is attainable by independent weak diagonal instruments.
\end{theorem}

The complete proof is in \cref{app:proofs}. Its logical sequence is worth stating. For each transcript, a Choi-positivity lemma upper-bounds recoverable EPR fidelity using only computational-basis likelihoods. Equation~\eqref{eq:factor} then makes the likelihood a product. Bayes' rule makes the posterior parity bias a product of $\delta_i$, while the recovery lemma makes EPR return a product of $f(\delta_i)$. An adaptive protocol is an average of realised strength profiles and cannot exceed the largest profile. Finally, the substitution $t_i=2y_i/(1+y_i^2)$ and strict convexity of $\log(1+e^{2z})$ show that equal $y_i$, hence equal $t_i$, optimise every linear support.

\begin{corollary}[Balanced null]
For $n\geq2$ and $\lambda=1/2$,
\[
\Cstream=\frac34.
\]
The least-disturbing optimum is $t=0$: the null learns no parity beyond chance and returns every EPR pair.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Closed forms and the strategy transition}

For one slot,
\[
C^{\mathrm{stream}}_{1,\lambda}
=\frac12+\frac12\sqrt{\lambda^2+(1-\lambda)^2}.
\]
For two slots,
\begin{equation}
C^{\mathrm{stream}}_{2,\lambda}=
\begin{cases}
1-\lambda/2,&0\leq\lambda\leq1/2,\\[1mm]
\lambda/2+\lambda^2/(3\lambda-1),&1/2<\lambda\leq1.
\end{cases}
\label{eq:n2}
\end{equation}
The optimal strength grows continuously from zero at $\lambda=1/2$.

For $n\geq3$, the transition is first order. Let $z_n$ be the unique solution
\begin{equation}
(1-z_n)(1+z_n)^{n-1}=1,qquad
z_n\in\left(\frac{n-2}{n},1\right).
\label{eq:zn}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
\lambda_c(n)=
\frac{1}{1+2^{n-2}z_n^{(n-2)/2}(1-z_n)}.
\label{eq:lambdac}
\end{equation}
Below $\lambda_c$, the optimum is $t=0$. At $\lambda_c$, it ties a finite-strength branch, so the optimal strength jumps discontinuously. Numerically,
\[
(\lambda_c,t_c)=
(0.6247789,0.9717365),
(0.6495455,0.9961752),
(0.6588955,0.9992936)
\]
for $n=3,4,5$. Moreover $\lambda_c(n)\to2/3$ from below. The optimal null thus changes from recording nothing to recording each slot almost projectively rather than moving through a broad weak-measurement phase.

\subsection{Collective classical-record comparator}

Now remove streaming sequestration and allow one joint instrument on all carriers, while requiring that only a classical outcome survives the commit point.

\begin{proposition}[Collective classical record]
For every $n$,
\begin{equation}
\Ccoll=\frac12+\frac12\sqrt{\lambda^2+(1-\lambda)^2}.
\label{eq:collective}
\end{equation}
For $n\geq2$ and $0<\lambda<1$, this is strictly larger than $\Cstream$.
\end{proposition}

A weak measurement of the two parity subspaces attains \eqref{eq:collective}. Optimality follows by grouping every outcome's likelihoods into even and odd sectors and applying the classical fidelity--total-variation circle; see \cref{app:proofs}. This comparator is not a coherent device: it retains only a classical outcome. It quantifies exactly what becomes possible if timing ceases to enforce causality.

\subsection{One coherent qubit closes the gap}

Initialise a memory qubit $M=\ket0$. In slot $i$, apply $\mathrm{CNOT}_{A_i\rightarrow M}$ and return $A_i$. After the final slot, commit $M$.
\begin{itemize}
  \item In AUDIT, measuring $M$ in $Z$ gives the parity of the later $Z$ measurements on the references.
  \item In RETURN, applying $\mathrm{CNOT}_{B_i\rightarrow M}$ for every sequestered carrier uncomputes $M$ to $\ket0$ and exactly restores all EPR pairs.
\end{itemize}
Thus $(\PA,\FR)=(1,1)$. The operational resource is a coherent temporal accumulator, not an observer or an interpretation of quantum mechanics.
